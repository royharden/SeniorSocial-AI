# LARGE-PRINT RESIDENT GUIDE

## Sign in

Sign-in APIs exist, but this build has no sign-in form page.
Ask your service organiser to help you enter your own account.
Never tell another person your sign-in code.

## Make the screen easier to use

1. Open **Settings**.
2. Choose **Easy** for larger words and buttons.
3. Confirm your display choice, then choose **English** or **Español**.
4. You can change these choices later.

## Find a service

1. Open the site's `/services` address: **Services directory**.
2. Under **Search services**, type what you need and press **Search**.
3. Read the details shown under each result.

If the AI helper is unavailable, directory search still works.
The site's `/help` address has the ordinary assistance form.

## Ask for help or a ride

1. Open the site's `/help` address for help or `/rides` for a ride.
2. Read the whole form.
3. Type or choose your information.
4. Review it.
5. Press the send button once.
6. Wait for the saved confirmation.

AI never sends a request for you. You decide when to send.

For an emergency, call **911**. Do not wait for SeniorSocial or an AI answer.

## Protect your privacy

- This build has no sign-out button. Do not use it on a shared device.
- Review each caregiver permission separately.
- You can remove a caregiver’s permission.
- Do not put private information in a community post.
