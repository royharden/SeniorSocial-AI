# LARGE-PRINT CAREGIVER GUIDE

## Set up help with the resident

The site's `/caregiver` page is for the resident to choose permissions.
It is called **Choose how someone can help**.

1. Ask the resident to use their own account.
2. They enter your email or phone under **Invite a caregiver**.
3. They press **Send invitation**.
4. Ask the service organiser to help finish acceptance.

This build has an acceptance API, but no acceptance page or resident chooser.
An invitation alone gives no access. Do not share sign-in codes.

You are acting as yourself, on behalf of the resident. You do not become the resident.

## Confirm exact permissions

The resident needs the caregiver link ID supplied by the setup workflow.
If it is missing, ask the service organiser for help.

1. The resident opens `/caregiver` and enters **Caregiver link ID**.
2. Read each selected permission aloud together.
3. The resident confirms the list and presses **Save exact permissions**.
4. Wait for **Your exact permissions were saved.**

Only the resident can confirm this list. They can use
**Revoke all caregiver access** to remove the link's permissions.

If AI is unavailable, permission setup still works. Not every resident form
supports caregiver actions: current intake accepts the resident's own account only.
Ask the service organiser for help with tasks without a supported caregiver screen.

## Important limits

- A permission can expire or be removed.
- One permission does not grant another.
- You cannot give the resident’s permission to someone else.
- Sign out on shared devices.
- For an emergency, call **911**.
